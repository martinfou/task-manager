# Risk Register

Lightweight risk tracking for the project. Update when new risks are identified or existing risks change.

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|----|------|--------|------------|------------|-------|--------|
| R-001 | [Example] Documentation drifts from code | High | Medium | Run Documentation-Code Consistency Check before commit | Team | Mitigated |
